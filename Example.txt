My database is used for one of BYU's research projects where student althetes take surveys each day and their responses are recorded.

Here is a question that worked:
question: "What schools have teams in the database?"
SQL: "SELECT DISTINCT `School` AS School\nFROM `Team`\nORDER BY `School`\nLIMIT 100"
Answer: "There are 2 schools with teams: byu and Test.\n\nThis list shows the distinct School values present in the database\u2019s teams data; the query returned both schools (no additional rows indicated)."
